description=[*][b]For every item of that kind, or just this one:[/b] [i]Every Whiskey[/i] covers every bottle you will ever carry; [i]Only this Whiskey[/i] covers that one bottle and wins over the other.
description=[*][b]Back to normal at any time:[/b] pick [i]The game's usual action[/i].
description=[*][b]Press again to put it away:[/b] when the action can't be done (the bat is already in your hand), the slot does the usual thing, which puts a held item away.
description=[*][b]Works with other mods:[/b] actions other mods add to an item's menu can be picked too, and items other mods make attachable (Plysken Attachments Reborn's bottles and pills) work as well.
